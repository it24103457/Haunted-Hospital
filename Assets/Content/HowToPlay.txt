=== Controls
WASD to move, mouse to look around.
Hold Shift to sprint, C to crouch, Space to jump.

E to interact: open doors, pick things up, read notes, use the keypad.
Left mouse to shoot, R to reload.

Esc to pause.

=== Health
model: Medkit
You lose health when something hurts you. If it runs out, you die.

Grab a Medkit to restore some of it. Walk up to one and press E to open it.

Supplies are scarce. Grab them before something else does.

=== Ammo
model: AmmoBox
Your gun reloads from the ammo you carry in reserve. Press R to reload once your magazine runs low.

Grab an Ammo Box to refill your reserve.

=== Sanity
model: SanityBottle
The hospital wears on your mind. Every time you catch sight of the Thief, your sanity slips. If it runs out, you lose your mind.

Grab a Sanity Bottle to steady it again. Its pills restore some of your sanity.

=== Clue Notes
model: CluePaper
Notes with single digits are scattered around the hospital. Each one gives you one number of the keypad code.

Your notes so far are shown at the top of the screen.

=== Keypad
model: Keypad
Enter the four-digit code from the notes to power the elevator. Type the digits and press Enter, or click the keys.

=== Exit Key
model: ExitKey
Locked away in one of the key boxes. You will need it for the elevator's key switch.

=== Key Box
model: KeyBox
Glass-fronted boxes on the walls. One of them holds the exit key.

=== Elevator Key Switch
model: KeySwitch
Your way out. With the power on and the exit key in hand, turn the key to escape.

=== The Thief
model: Thief
Something else is in here, and it wants your supplies. It sneaks to medkits, ammo and pills, carries them off to its stash and vanishes, only to return later.

Just seeing it chips away at your sanity. Shoot it to make it drop what it is carrying. It goes for what you need most, so do not leave supplies lying around.

=== Thief Stashes
model: StashBag
Bags and baskets where the Thief hides what it takes. Once an item reaches a stash, it is gone.

=== Barricades
model: Barricade
Some doorways are boarded up. Shoot the planks off one at a time to get through.

Breaking the last plank is loud. Something may come to see what made the noise.
